Who It Can Help

  • Deaf and hard-of-hearing people in daily conversations.
  • Hospitals and clinics where an interpreter may not be available.
  • Schools, colleges and public service centres.

These are intended use cases, not claims of existing deployments.

About

SignBridge AI is a hackathon prototype. It combines hand tracking with a model trained on a small, defined Indian Sign Language vocabulary, plus speech tools and emergency phrases. Continuous signing, grammar and facial expressions are future scope.
